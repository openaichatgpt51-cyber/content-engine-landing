How it works

From your first post to a full calendar in four steps.

01

Teach it your voice

Paste posts you love, upload examples, or import from your connected accounts. It builds a style guide for each platform.

02

Choose a topic

Type one topic or plan a whole campaign. It researches, writes the article, then adapts it for LinkedIn, Instagram and X.

03

Review and approve

Posts arrive with images, ready to read. Edit, reject or approve. You decide what represents your brand.

04

It posts and improves

Approved posts go out at your chosen times. Your edits and results feed the next round, so drafts need less fixing.

Features

Built so your content sounds like you, not like a template.

Learns your brand voice

A style guide per platform, built from your own posts. LinkedIn reads differently from Instagram and X, and the engine writes each one that way.

22 languages, per brand

Pick the language and country for each brand and every post is written for those readers. Serve a client in Lagos and another in Abidjan from one account.

Approval first, always

Every post waits for your OK, and a pause switch holds the whole account whenever you need it. No surprises on your company page.

Many brands, one login

For agencies and groups. Each brand gets its own voice, schedule, connected accounts and daily limits, so clients never get mixed up.

Images that fit the post

Set your colours, font and logo once. Each post gets a visual in your brand's look, with the headline placed on it and editable in the queue.

Schedule and publish

Pick your posting days and time once. Approved posts are queued on a calendar and published straight to your connected accounts.
