Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

ContentEngine ("we", "us") is a product of Greenatech Global, based in Lagos, Nigeria. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit contentengine.social or use the ContentEngine app, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Demo requests: your name, work email, company, number of brands and any message you send.
  • Account data: your email, company details, brand settings (description, audience, tone, language, country, posting schedule), and brand images and reference posts you upload.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect LinkedIn, Instagram or X, we store the access tokens and basic profile details those platforms provide so we can publish the posts you approve and read analytics for your own posts.
  • Content: the topics you enter and the posts, images and edits generated and approved in the app.
  • Billing: we currently invoice customers directly and do not collect card payments through the platform, so we do not hold card details. If online card payments are introduced, they will be handled by a payment provider and this policy will be updated.

How we use it

  • To reply to demo requests and provide the service you signed up for.
  • To generate content in your brand's voice and publish only what you approve.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse.

Who processes data for us

We use service providers to run ContentEngine: Supabase (database and file storage), Vercel (hosting), n8n (workflow automation), OpenAI (text and image generation and analysis) and Resend (email). Your content and brand settings are sent to the AI provider only to produce the content you request. We do not sell your personal information.

Social platforms

Our use of data from LinkedIn, Instagram and X follows each platform's developer terms. You can disconnect an account in Settings at any time, and you can revoke access from within the platform itself.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app, which removes your brands, posts, uploads and connections. You can also ask us to delete your data, or demo-request details, by emailing hello@contentengine.social.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, as applicable under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that apply to you.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app uses essential cookies to keep you signed in.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@contentengine.social.
